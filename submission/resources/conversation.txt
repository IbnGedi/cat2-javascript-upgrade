I interviewed Stephen Gichau, a staff member at REV Virtual Racing.

Stephen explained that the company offers Formula 1 simulator experiences 
and go-kart racing activities for racing enthusiasts, gamers, families, 
and groups of friends. He said the main purpose of the website is to 
showcase the racing experiences available and help customers make 
inquiries and bookings.

Stephen preferred a modern racing-themed design using black, red, and 
white colors. He also wanted the website to feature images of the Formula 
1 simulators and go-kart activities so visitors can easily understand 
what REV Virtual Racing offers.

REV Virtual Racing is Nairobi's premier motorsport entertainment destination. We bring the thrill of Formula 1 racing and go-kart action to racing enthusiasts, gamers, families, and groups of friends.

Whether you're a seasoned racing fan or trying it for the first time, our state-of-the-art simulators and kart tracks deliver an unforgettable experience for everyone.
